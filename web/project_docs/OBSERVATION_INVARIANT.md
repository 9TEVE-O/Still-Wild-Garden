## Observation Invariant

**Observation must never regenerate the world. The garden can change only because time or an authorised garden event changed it.**

Opening, refreshing, logging in, logging out, reconnecting, changing devices, resizing the display, or otherwise viewing the garden are observation events only.

Observation may reconstruct and render the current garden from its canonical identity, history and state, but it must not create new world state, relocate persistent features, replace landmarks, reseed terrain, or otherwise alter garden reality.

A visible change must always be attributable to one of two causes:

1. **Time** — deterministic growth, ageing, seasons or other explicitly time-derived processes.
2. **Authorised garden events** — recorded actions or consequences permitted by the garden’s rules.

If neither occurred, the same garden state must reconstruct to the same persistent world.

Major landmarks and persistent spatial relationships are facts about the garden, not disposable outputs of a rendering session.
